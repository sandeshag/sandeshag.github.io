About Me

Grounded in soil science, driven by community impact

I'm a soil scientist based in Nepal with a Master's degree in Soil Science from the Agriculture and Forestry University, Rampur. I currently serve as Soil Scientist and Office Chief at the Soil and Fertilizer Testing Laboratory in Rajbiraj, Saptari, under the Ministry of Land Management, Agriculture and Cooperative, Madhesh Province.

My work sits at the intersection of soil health, soil biology, and sustainable agriculture — from studying earthworm diversity across land-use patterns to championing vermicomposting and integrated nutrient management for smallholder farmers. Alongside my scientific work, I co-founded Team Vermiponics, an organic agriculture startup, and serve as the Founding Secretary of Dream100, a volunteer-based NGO that helps low-income students pursue their college dreams.

I believe good soil stewardship and youth empowerment go hand in hand — both require patience, evidence, and a long-term view.
